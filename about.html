Who I Am

About Me

From managing Facebook pages to building full-stack marketing systems—my career has been a deliberate progression from execution to infrastructure.

I started in digital marketing in 2013, managing social media pages and local campaigns. What pulled me deeper wasn’t the content—it was the data behind it. Why did one post outperform another? Why did a campaign with half the budget sometimes deliver twice the results?

That curiosity drove me from social media into performance marketing, then into technical SEO, analytics infrastructure, and marketing automation. Over six years and 150+ projects, I’ve worked across fintech, e-commerce, B2B SaaS, and professional services—helping businesses build marketing systems that compound over time, not just campaigns that spike and fade.

Today I specialise in the intersection of data, performance, and automation. I set up GA4 and GTM the right way, build Google and Meta campaigns that optimise for revenue (not just clicks), implement technical SEO that actually improves rankings, and design CRM workflows that turn leads into customers on autopilot.

What makes me different is that I operate across the full stack. I can audit your tracking, fix the data, run the campaigns, and report on what matters—all without the hand-off overhead of working with five different specialists.
