Who I Am

About Me

Digital Marketing & Marketing Technology Specialist with 10+ years of experience across performance marketing, analytics, CRM automation, and AI-assisted MarTech workflows—building systems that generate qualified leads, not just traffic.

I started in digital marketing in 2013, managing Facebook pages and supporting local television campaigns at a regional ad agency. What began as content publishing evolved into a deeper interest in how marketing actually performs—the data behind it, the tracking that makes decisions possible, and the systems that make growth repeatable.

From 2015 to 2020 at Giri Trading Agency, I built the full digital marketing function—SEO, PPC, Google Shopping, and Facebook campaigns across Indian, US, and Canadian markets. I scaled ad spend from ₹0.5L to ₹3L/month while maintaining profitability, and implemented Universal Analytics with proper goal and conversion tracking.

At Peppers Media (2021–2022), I managed PPC campaigns and Facebook lead-generation targeting the US B2B market, generating qualified leads and building client pitch decks for agency relationships.

From March 2022 to March 2026, at Ark Neo Financial Services (DhanLAP), I operated across the full marketing stack: Google Ads Search campaigns targeting high-intent financial audiences, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, AEO using schema markup and structured data (FAQ, HowTo, Article), GA4 and GTM implementation, full CRM setup with automation flows, and SQL-based reporting dashboards in Looker Studio.

Since April 2026, I’ve been Deputy Manager – Marketing at SolidPro Engineering Support, working at the intersection of digital marketing, marketing technology, analytics, and automation across multiple B2B verticals. I’ve built internal tools including a UTM generation system, a dataLayer generation workflow for GA4/GTM, a LinkedIn auto-posting workflow, and AI-assisted MCP workflows for Google Ads, Saleshandy, and Zoho CRM — connecting marketing, CRM, analytics, and technology teams.

My approach: data infrastructure first, then execution. Clean tracking before campaigns. Proper attribution before budget decisions. Automation before scale.
