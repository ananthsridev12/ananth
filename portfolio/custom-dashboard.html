The Challenge

DhanLAP's marketing reporting was manual and spread across just 2 channels, with the team spending around 4 hours a week pulling data by hand instead of running campaigns.

What I Did

  • Unified Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, GA4, and CRM data into a single reporting layer
  • Wrote SQL queries to aggregate campaign data across all 5 channels
  • Built executive dashboards in Looker Studio for campaign reporting
  • Scaled tracked campaign coverage from 12 to 35+ campaigns as channels were unified

Outcome

Weekly reporting time dropped from 4 hours to 30 minutes. Channel coverage in reporting grew from 2 to 5, and tracked campaigns grew from 12 to 35+, giving the team a single source of truth instead of manual data pulls.
