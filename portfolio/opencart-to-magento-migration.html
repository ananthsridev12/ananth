The Challenge

Giri Trading Agency's e-commerce catalogue needed to move off OpenCart onto Magento for better platform capability and scale. Platform migrations are one of the highest-risk SEO events - a mishandled URL structure, missing redirects, or lost metadata can wipe out years of organic rankings overnight.

What I Did

  • Audited and mapped every indexed OpenCart URL to its new Magento equivalent
  • Implemented 301 redirects for the full URL map ahead of launch
  • Rebuilt and resubmitted the XML sitemap for the new Magento structure
  • Set canonical tags to prevent duplicate content issues between old and new URL patterns
  • Preserved existing meta titles, descriptions, and schema markup through the migration
  • Ran a full pre-launch crawl to catch broken links and redirect chains before go-live
  • Monitored Google Search Console daily post-launch to validate indexation and catch ranking drops early

Outcome

The migration went live with the full URL map redirected and no loss in search rankings - the primary goal of any SEO-first platform move. The site continued running on Magento with its existing organic visibility intact, clearing the way for further SEO and paid growth work covered in the E-Commerce SEO Overhaul case study.
