The Challenge

Loan Against Mutual Funds is a considered, research-heavy purchase—most prospective borrowers search for their specific AMC or mutual fund scheme long before they search for a loan product. DhanLAP had no content presence to capture that top-of-funnel intent or a way to bring those researchers back once they'd shown interest.

What I Did

  • Built a programmatic template generating 3,000+ pages targeting individual AMC and scheme keywords
  • Drove qualified visits to these pages primarily through paid campaigns
  • Built a 52K-strong retargeting audience from users who engaged with the mutual fund pages
  • Layered Loan Against Mutual Funds (LAMF) retargeting campaigns on top of that audience
  • Tracked assisted conversions in GA4 to attribute LAMF leads back to the programmatic pages

Outcome

The programmatic page set built a 52K-user retargeting pool of people actively researching mutual funds. Retargeting that audience with LAMF offers drove a 32% increase in assisted LAMF leads.
